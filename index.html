What am I watching?

This is a replay of actual decisions and neural spike counts from the frozen Step 5 experiment, not a new training run. A fixed visual adapter selects the input cells. The brain close-up preserves the neuron cell-body coordinates supplied with the MaleCNS dataset. Faint dots are anatomical context, not measured silence: per-cell activity is available here only for the selected input cells and six readout neurons. One selected input cell has no recorded position and is excluded from the spatial overlay. The fly body is AI illustrated, not a registered anatomical scan. Optional straight links show known synaptic connectivity, not the paths of neurites or measured causal transmission.

Learning took place in existing fly-connectome synapses. The board scan, visual recognition, and Undo stack are supplied by the experiment. Readout activity is measured over 500 ms per decision; no rewards or weight changes occur during this replay. The score is mean MBON11 − mean MBON07 − 1.625 Hz: at least +2 accepts, at most −2 rejects, and values between time out.

The trained policy solved 158 of 160 reserved puzzles. A conservative control solved all 160 by avoiding ambiguous choices, so successful recovery does not establish superior general Sudoku reasoning.
